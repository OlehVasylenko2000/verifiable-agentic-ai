# Verifiable Agentic AI

Trust, accountability and provenance for autonomous AI agents.

## About

AI agents increasingly act directly: they initiate transactions, access systems, delegate to other agents and take consequential decisions with limited human supervision at the moment of action. This breaks the assumption that behind every consequential action there is an identifiable human or registered legal entity.

This repository is the public working space of Oleh Vasylenko around three questions that currently have no infrastructural answer: who an agent is, on whose authority it acts, and what it actually did — and a fourth: what an inclusive trust layer requires so that institutions outside the largest platforms and regulatory blocs can take part on equal terms.

## Where this continues

The ITU-T Focus Group on Trust and Identity for Humans and Agentic AI (FG-TIDA) is open to anyone, free of charge, without ITU membership. Its candidate themes are developed in public on GitHub.

A related open candidate theme there is **Accountability and attribution after an agent has acted**, proposed by the organizer of this session. Contributions are welcome before and after the session: https://github.com/FG-TIDA/themes/issues/1
